## What scoring missed (root causes)

1. **Ask-sweep + tide is necessary but not sufficient.** Those chips stay. They must not keep a row on Picks / Premove after the grace window if nothing confirmed.
2. **No post-print invalidation.** Later bid dumps and −15% live fades existed; “never confirmed” did not. Same-day losers and the flat put pile sat on the book as high-conviction.
3. **Watches did not expire.** Age scored the *alert* down; the *watch row* still looked live. MMM-class calls needed an expire / “thesis fading” state.
4. **`all_opening_trades` was too loud.** Winners MU / INTC were not all-opening. Do not treat missing opening as a fade risk.
5. **Moneyness unused.** Strike vs spot was on the alert and never scored. 350P vs 360P is the exhibit.

## Desk changes that follow (hypotheses, verified on local fixtures)

- After **2 hours**, require confirming ask-side flow on the same chain (or ticker + side) **or** a later print / historic last that is actually up. Otherwise **one-and-done (−14)** and drop from Picks / Premove.
- Confirmed follow-through: **+5**. Fresh prints inside the grace window stay pending — do not dock them for being new.
- Soften all-opening **+10 → +4**. Remove “not all-opening” as a fade-risk line.
- Near-ATM / modest OTM **+3**; deep ITM **−4 / −8**; far OTM **−6**.
- Watches older than **one session** with no premium follow-through: **expire**. Aged **calls** without follow-through: hard expire (MMM class). Historic path on the 15-minute watch check can confirm GH-class (premium still working).
- Keep boosting fresh ask-sweep + tide. Do not treat those chips as enough alone.
